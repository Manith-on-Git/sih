## 2. Mandatory Declarations (Rule 6 - Verified Primary Provisions)
Under **Rule 6(1)** of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, every package shall bear thereon the following declarations:

1. **Name and Address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer**:
   - Rule reference: `Rule 6(1)(a)`
   - Status: VERIFIED
   - Requirement: Name and complete address of the manufacturer, or where the manufacturer is not the packer, the name and address of the manufacturer and packer, or for imported packages, the name and address of the importer.

2. **Common or Generic Name of Commodity**:
   - Rule reference: `Rule 6(1)(b)`
   - Status: VERIFIED
   - Requirement: The common or generic names of the commodity contained in the package and in case of packages with more than one product, the name and quantity of each product.

3. **Net Quantity**:
   - Rule reference: `Rule 6(1)(c)`
   - Status: VERIFIED
   - Requirement: Net quantity in terms of standard unit of weight or measure, or number of commodity contained in the package.

4. **Month and Year of Manufacture / Packing / Import**:
   - Rule reference: `Rule 6(1)(d)`
   - Status: VERIFIED
   - Requirement: The month and year in which the commodity is manufactured or packed or imported.

5. **Maximum Retail Price (MRP)**:
   - Rule reference: `Rule 6(1)(e)`
   - Status: VERIFIED
   - Requirement: Retail sale price of the package clearly indicating "Maximum Retail Price / MRP Rs. ... / ₹ ... inclusive of all taxes".
